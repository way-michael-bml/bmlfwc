Suggest a Task

Have an idea for something that should be in our Action Plan? Tell us here instead of by email so ideas don't get lost in someone's inbox. Name and parcel address are required so we know suggestions are coming from verified neighbors.

Think neighborhood-wide, not just your own lot — shared infrastructure and community-wide projects belong in a 3-year plan just as much as individual tasks. For example:

  • Remove non-cosmetic brush and ladder fuels within 100 ft. of all roads, driveways, and structures.
  • Add water storage tanks and hydrants so firefighters have what they need to defend our homes.
  • Accelerated forest rehabilitation: thin overcrowded, small-diameter stands and widen tree spacing for a healthier, genuinely fire-safe forest.
