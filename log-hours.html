Log Your Hours & Expenses

Two minutes now keeps our Firewise recognition on track

← Back to Documents

To keep our official Firewise USA recognition each year, our neighborhood needs to collectively log a minimum amount of volunteer time and/or money spent on wildfire safety work — see our FAQ for what counts. If you cleared brush, limbed a tree, cleaned gutters, or paid a contractor for fuel reduction, log it below. This log is for our internal recognition tracking only and won’t be shared publicly.
